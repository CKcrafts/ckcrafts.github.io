Privacy Policy for Interactive Countdowns

Overview: Interactive Countdowns ("we", "our") is developed by Juan Sebastian Camacho (CK Crafts). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Information Collection and Storage: Interactive Countdowns does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, tracking data, or user content on external servers. All countdown timer details, names, and user preferences created within the app remain stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services: The app utilizes Google Play Services and Google Play Billing to process optional in-app purchases and manage app licensing. Any payment information entered during a transaction is handled securely and directly by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy.
